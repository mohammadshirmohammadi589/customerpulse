# CustomerPulse Metric Dictionary

## 1. Customer Count

**Definition:** Number of distinct customers with at least one qualifying transaction.

**Formula:**

`COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id)`

**Grain:** Customer

**Time Window:** Full analysis period

**Interpretation:** Number of customers represented in the qualified analytical dataset.

**Limitation:** Customers without qualifying transactions are excluded.

---

## 2. Transaction Count

**Definition:** Number of distinct qualifying transactions.

**Formula:**

`COUNT(DISTINCT transaction_id)`

**Grain:** Transaction

**Interpretation:** Number of completed/qualified purchase transactions represented in the dataset.

**Important:** The source dataset may contain multiple line items per transaction. Therefore transaction count must not be calculated from raw row count.

---

## 3. Revenue

**Definition:** Sum of qualifying transaction amounts.

**Formula:**

`SUM(amount)`

**Grain:** Transaction

**Interpretation:** Observed revenue represented by qualifying transactions.

**Limitation:** The MVP does not perform multi-currency conversion.

---

## 4. Recency

**Definition:** Number of days since the customer's most recent qualifying transaction.

**Formula:**

`analysis_date - last_transaction_date`

**Grain:** Customer

**Interpretation:** Lower values indicate more recent activity.

**Limitation:** Recency does not represent churn probability.

---

## 5. Frequency

**Definition:** Number of distinct qualifying transactions associated with a customer.

**Formula:**

`COUNT(DISTINCT transaction_id)`

**Grain:** Customer

**Interpretation:** Higher values indicate more frequent historical purchasing.

---

## 6. Monetary

**Definition:** Total observed revenue generated by a customer.

**Formula:**

`SUM(amount)`

**Grain:** Customer

**Interpretation:** Higher values indicate greater historical monetary contribution.

---

## 7. RFM Score

**Definition:** Relative customer scoring based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.

**Scoring method:**

Customers are divided into five relative groups using `NTILE(5)`.

* Recency: lower is better
* Frequency: higher is better
* Monetary: higher is better

Each dimension receives a score from 1 to 5.

**Grain:** Customer

**Example:**

`555` represents a customer in the highest relative group for all three dimensions.

**Limitation:** Quintile scores are relative to the analyzed population and are not universal business thresholds. Ties and small datasets can affect the exact allocation.

---

## 8. Historical Customer Value

**Definition:** Total observed qualifying revenue generated by a customer during the available historical period.

**Formula:**

`SUM(amount)`

**Grain:** Customer

**Important:** This is historical realized value, not predictive Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

**Limitation:** It does not estimate future customer revenue.

---

## 9. Cohort

**Definition:** Customers grouped by the month of their first qualifying transaction.

**Formula:**

`DATE_TRUNC('month', first_transaction_date)`

**Grain:** Customer cohort

**Interpretation:** Allows customers acquired in different periods to be compared based on elapsed time since acquisition.

---

## 10. Cohort Retention

**Definition:** Percentage of customers from a cohort who have qualifying activity in a given elapsed month.

**Formula:**

`retained_customers / cohort_size`

**Grain:** Cohort × Period

**Interpretation:** Measures customer retention based on observed purchase activity.

**Important:** This is customer retention, not transaction retention.

**NULL periods:** A NULL future period means that the period has not yet been observed in the available dataset. It should not automatically be interpreted as zero retention.
