# Survicate Customer Health Mini Audit

## Objective

This proof of concept demonstrates how customer engagement,
feedback signals, and historical customer value can be combined
into a transparent customer-health framework.

## Dataset

The analysis uses 20 synthetic customers.

It does not use or claim access to Survicate internal data.

## Key Findings

- 7 customers generated a HIGH risk signal.
- 13 customers were classified as LOW priority.
- No customers were classified as MEDIUM priority in this synthetic scenario.
- High-priority customers combine significant engagement decline
  with relatively high historical customer value.

## Business Interpretation

The framework is designed to help Customer Success teams identify
customers who may deserve attention based on observable behavioral
signals.

The output is a prioritization signal, not a churn prediction.

## Recommended Workflow

Customer engagement
→ customer health
→ transparent priority
→ Customer Success review
→ targeted follow-up
→ feedback collection

## Limitations

- Synthetic data
- Heuristic rules
- No churn model
- No predictive CLV
- No access to internal Survicate data
- Thresholds would require validation with real historical outcomes

## Potential Next Step

With appropriate internal data, the framework could be evaluated
against historical customer outcomes and refined using validated
business thresholds.
